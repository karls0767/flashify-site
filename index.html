Flashcards made for you, printed and posted

Flashify turns any topic, or your own notes, into a deck of 10 to 50 study flashcards. Edit the cards, choose a design, and we'll print them and post them to you in the UK or US.

How it works

  1. Type a topic, or upload a PDF, photo or text file of your notes.
  2. Review your cards. Edit or remove any you don't want.
  3. Pick a card colour, text colour and font.
  4. Check out securely with Stripe. We print your deck and post it to you.
