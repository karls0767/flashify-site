Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Flashify app ("Flashify", "we", "us") collects, why, and what we do with it. Flashify is run by Karl Searles, trading as Flashify, based in the United Kingdom. Karl Searles is the data controller for your information. Contact us at info@flashifyapp.com.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we use itHow long we keep it
Topics you type and files you upload (PDFs, photos, text) To generate your flashcards. They are sent to our AI provider for processing. We do not store your uploaded files or topics. They are used only to create your cards.
An anonymous app ID and a record of when you generate a deck To apply fair-use limits and stop abuse of the service. This ID is not linked to your name or email. Generation records are kept for up to 90 days.
Order details: your cards, chosen design, name, email address and delivery address To print and post your order, send updates, and handle any problems or refunds. As long as needed to fulfil your order and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements (usually six years).
Payment information Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details. Stripe keeps payment records under its own policy.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and Flashify has no third-party ads or tracking.

Legal basis (UK and EU users)

We process your information to provide the service and fulfil your order (performance of a contract), to prevent abuse (our legitimate interests), and to keep records we are required to keep by law (legal obligation).

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process information on our behalf:

  • Anthropic (AI provider): receives your topic or uploaded file to generate flashcards. Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Supabase (database and servers, hosted in the EU): stores orders and generation records. Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Stripe (payments): processes your payment and collects your delivery address. Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Printing and postal services: receive what is needed to produce and deliver your order.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where your information is transferred outside the UK or EU, it is protected by appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to how we use it, or for a copy of it. Email info@flashifyapp.com. We may need to keep some order records where the law requires it. If you are in the UK and unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Flashify is intended for students aged 13 and over. Anyone under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before placing an order. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
